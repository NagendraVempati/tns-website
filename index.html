04 GOOD QUESTIONS. CLEAR ANSWERS.

A little clarity
before we connect.

Planning a new connection or changing an existing one? Start here.

What types of projects do you cover?+

Our focus is electrical HT and LT line works, existing line shifting, underground cable works, and 11 kV / 33 kV substation infrastructure for residential developments, commercial sites and industry.

Do you work on household connections?+

Household and building requirements are covered through LT electrical distribution and cabling. HT networks and 11 kV / 33 kV infrastructure relate to larger developments, industrial sites and their incoming supply.

Can existing overhead lines be shifted?+

Line shifting can be considered for construction, road changes and site redevelopment. The scope depends on the existing network, the proposed route and the relevant utility permissions. Start by sharing the location and the reason for relocation.

What should I include in my project enquiry?+

Share the project location, the type of property, the service you need and your preferred timeline. If available, note the voltage level, approximate route length and whether this is new work or a change to an existing connection.
